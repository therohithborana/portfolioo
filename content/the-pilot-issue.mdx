## Then I watched Nimsdai

I came across Nimsdai on social media this week and ended up going down a bit of a rabbit hole.

I watched his conversation with Joe Rogan and then watched *14 Peaks: Nothing Is Impossible*.

If you don’t know him (just like me previously), Nirmal Purja was a mountaineer who climbed all 14 of the world’s 8,000-metre peaks in just **6 months and 6 days**, the previous record was just under 8 years! He left the British Special Forces to pursue mountaineering and also had to figure out how to fund the whole project. The guy and his team shot **100+ hours of footage** while doing all of this as well!

![Nimsdai](/pilot-issue-nimsdai.jpg)

Seeing him really made me wanna go to Pokhara right away!\
I don’t know when I’ll go but I’ll definitely go.

And I think that’s what stayed with me after watching him.

**“Do things anyways!”**

Maybe at 22, I should be doing more things simply because I want to.
